    CATCH: A Controllable Analysis Testbed for Reward Hacking in Coding RL

    Shouli Wang, Yanfeng Jia, Zhihao Ou, Zitao Su, Ruize He, Haotong Xie, Hao Peng, Juanzi Li, Xiaozhi Wang

    Xuekang Wang, Zhuoyuan Hao, Shuo Hou, Hao Peng, Juanzi Li, Xiaozhi Wang

    arXiv:2606.04923 · 2026

    在 CHERRL 实验环境中，研究者给负责按细则评分的语言模型引入已知偏差，再观察受训模型何时学会利用这些偏差得分，以及能否从训练日志中识别这一变化。

    Teach the Way It Thinks: Guiding LLM Post-training Data Engineering with Sparse Autoencoders

    Yi Jing, Zao Dai, Jinwu Hu, Zijun Yao, Lei Hou, Juanzi Li, Xiaozhi Wang

    arXiv:2605.27354 · 2026

    SAERL 用稀疏自编码器识别每道数学题会激活模型内部的哪些特征，再据此选择训练题目、安排练习顺序，并决定哪些题目放在同一批次训练。
