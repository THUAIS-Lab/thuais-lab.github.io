Tsinghua University

Artificial Intelligence
Science Group

We study how large models work and how they learn. Led by Xiaozhi Wang at the Shenzhen International Graduate School, our group explores how these models can contribute to scientific discovery.

Research

We study how language models represent information and use it to make predictions. We trace these computations inside the model and use what we learn to choose training examples, design rewards, and evaluate what a model has learned during post-training. We also investigate how AI systems can propose and test ideas in scientific research.
